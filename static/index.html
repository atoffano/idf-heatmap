Methodology & Open Data Pipeline

Travel times are computed from Sorbonne Université — Campus Pierre et Marie Curie (7-9 quai Saint Bernard, 75005 Paris) across Paris and Petite Couronne using a Hybrid Street-Graph Dijkstra + Transit Station Propagation pipeline:

  1. OpenStreetMap Street & Cycleway Network: All walkable streets, bridges over the Seine, footpaths, steps, and cycleways are parsed into weighted graphs. Every street intersection (crossroad) is identified as a routing target.
  2. Exact Pedestrian & Bicycle Dijkstra: Single-source shortest paths compute exact door-to-door times for walking (4.8 km/h, step penalties) and cycling (18.5 km/h on cycleways, 15.5 km/h on streets, dismount on pedestrian/steps), strictly respecting physical barriers like the Seine bridges.
  3. Public Services Graph + Pedestrian Propagation: Métro, RER, Transilien, Tramway, and Bus route sequences are connected to street nodes with realistic boarding/wait times, dwell times, and commercial speeds. Dijkstra propagates station arrival times outward along the pedestrian street graph to every crossroad.
  4. Metric IDW Interpolation & Contours: Crossroad travel times are interpolated onto a 200×200 spatial grid using local metric inverse-distance weighting (cKDTree) and converted into 5–60 minute GeoJSON isochrones.
